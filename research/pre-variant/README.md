# The pre-variant `project-relay` skill

Two snapshots of the single generic skill that existed before the split into project-type
variants. `skills/project-relay-git/` is their Git-scoped successor. **Neither is installable and
neither is maintained** — they are kept because `research/iteration-1/` and `research/iteration-2/`
grade outputs produced by exactly these files, and without them those benchmarks cannot be read
back to a cause.

| Directory | What it is |
|---|---|
| `iteration-1-skill/` | The first draft. Produced the `with_skill` arm of `research/iteration-1/`, and the `old_skill` baseline arm of `research/iteration-2/`. |
| `iteration-2-skill/` | The revision. Produced the `with_skill` arm of `research/iteration-2/`. The last state of the skill before the variant split. |

`scripts/handoff_context.py` is byte-identical in both — the iteration-2 revision changed only
`SKILL.md`, the references, the templates, and the evals.

## Reading the diff between them

`research/README.md` explains what the iteration-2 revision was trying to fix (length, not
accuracy) and what it left unfixed (blocks duplicated across both documents). The two directories
here are that revision as a diff you can actually run:

```bash
diff -ru research/pre-variant/iteration-1-skill research/pre-variant/iteration-2-skill
```

Note that `iteration-2-skill/` still carries the character-count caps in its evals. Those were
deliberately **not** carried into `project-relay-git` — they penalise complex projects for being
complex. See `skills/project-relay-git/CHANGELOG.md`.

## What is not archived

The fixture projects these ran against are not kept, in either their workspace copies or the
per-eval copies. They are regenerated by `research/build_fixtures.py`, which builds all three
(`a-webapp-session`, `b-master-update`, `c-nontechnical-full`) including their Git history. The
`review.html` files from the eval viewer are not kept either; `generate_review.py` in the
`skill-creator` skill rebuilds them from the surrounding `benchmark.json` and `grading.json`.
